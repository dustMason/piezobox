RATE BOUNDARY
The original firmware passed 8 kHz/input and overloaded at 12 kHz/input.
V2 fast passed short tests through 39 kHz/input. At 40 and 44.1 kHz, frames
were dropped; at 48 kHz and higher the acquisition overrun guard stopped it.
V2 settled passed 13 kHz/input in a five-second run; 14 kHz lost frames.
32 kHz fast was selected by the user for the app and normal operation.

METHOD AND SCOPE
Physical Teensy LC, 48 MHz, USB Serial, inputs 15–21. The long receiver tests
parsed sequence/counter packets without per-sample WAV work in the hot path.
They used open-ended STREAM commands and host STOP, with no fixed frame limit.
Continuous-run code removes the old 600-second command limit, but these runs
do not establish indefinite reliability or >71-minute micros-wrap behavior.
The seven jack/piezo assignments are not fully mapped; this tests seven ADC
inputs, not proof of seven connected and independently identified transducers.
Fast mode uses short sample windows without dummy conversions. Source settling,
analog crosstalk, hum, clipping, protection, and anti-aliasing are unqualified.
The LC multiplexes one ADC; the seven values in a frame are not simultaneous.
The existing circuit cannot capture the clipped negative half of the waveform.
