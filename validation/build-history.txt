The LC now exposes USB Audio Class 1 mono and seven-channel inputs, both 32 kHz
and 16-bit signed PCM sourced from ADC12. It also retains CDC serial diagnostics
and software reboot. macOS enumerates them as Piezo Box LC Mono and
Piezo Box LC 7 Inputs through its built-in AppleUSBAudio driver.
No Mac HAL driver was installed and no system audio service restart was needed.

Real PCM from the piezos was recorded through both inputs. Digital-pattern
checks qualify channel ordering and continuity separately from analog signal
quality. A simultaneous 120-second mono + seven-channel run checked 3,834,368
frames per input, with no sequence/sample errors, ADC faults, late ticks, or
streaming underruns/overruns. A further 30-second check of the permanent build
checked 954,368 seven-channel frames with the same clean result. Expected startup
silence/discards are reported separately. Diagnostic patterns were turned off.

The release has no prototype reboot timer. Multiple USB-only updates succeeded
without reaching the enclosed Program button. The original and v2 serial HEX
files remain unchanged as rollback snapshots.
